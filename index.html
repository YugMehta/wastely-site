Common questions

Which items can Wastely recognize?

Wastely classifies items from what the camera sees, which means it works on unbranded, damaged, and mixed-material items that barcode lookups miss. Current sorting accuracy is 94%.

How does it handle different local rules?

Disposal law varies by state and county. Wastely checks each item against the rules for the location where it is thrown away and returns an answer specific to that place.

What do the reports include?

You get running ESG metrics, an audit trail of every sort, and a compliance report for each jurisdiction you operate in. The dashboard also tracks waste diverted and the related water and carbon data.

Who is Wastely built for?

Wastely is built for organizations that manage shared waste streams, including hospitals, universities, corporate offices, and recycling facilities.
