How Wastely works.

Three stages connect the moment an item is thrown away to the report your compliance team files.

Book a demo
01

It sees the item, not the barcode.

Computer vision identifies the material and its condition in real time, at 94% sorting accuracy. Because it works from the image, it handles the items that cause the most mistakes.

  • Unbranded packaging with no label to look up
  • Damaged or food-soiled items
  • Mixed-material items

02

It applies the law where you are.

Disposal law varies by state and county. Wastely checks every item against the rules for the place it is thrown away and answers instantly, which means nobody has to interpret a sign above the bin.

03

It writes the record for you.

Every sort becomes a line in your audit trail. The dashboard turns those events into running ESG metrics and a compliance report for each jurisdiction you operate in.
